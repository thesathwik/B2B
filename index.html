Privacy policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Vaada runs a WhatsApp service that helps small businesses in India send bills to their buyers and get paid on time. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Your WhatsApp phone number and profile name.
  • Messages, photos, documents and voice notes you send to our WhatsApp number.
  • Business details you give us, such as business name, address, GSTIN or PAN, and bank account details.
  • Bill details: amounts, due dates, items, and whether a bill was accepted, disputed or paid.
  • Records of what you approved and when, such as accepting a bill or approving an automatic payment.

How we use it

  • To send bills, reminders and receipts between sellers and buyers.
  • To set up and collect payments the buyer has approved, through our payment partner.
  • To keep an accurate record of every bill and payment.
  • To read bill photos and voice notes automatically so you don't have to type.

Who we share it with

  • The other business on a bill (the seller or the buyer) sees that bill and its status.
  • Service providers that help us run the service: Meta (WhatsApp), our payment partner, cloud hosting, and AI services that read bills and voice notes. They may only use the data to provide their service to us.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

We do not sell your data.

Where it is stored and for how long

Data is stored on servers in India. We keep bill and payment records for as long as the law requires for financial records, and other data only as long as needed to provide the service.

Your choices

You can ask us to show, correct or delete your data, or stop using the service at any time, by writing to bethisathwikreddy@gmail.com. Some payment records must be kept by law even after you leave.
